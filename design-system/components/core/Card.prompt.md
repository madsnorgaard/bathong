The print card - a paper panel that lifts off a colour ground with a hard ink shadow. Used for the featured walk, pull-outs and any panel sitting on jacaranda.

```jsx
<Card><h3 className="b-display-2">Photowalk № 1 - The Layers Route</h3></Card>
```

Never rounded, never soft-shadowed. On paper grounds prefer a plain bordered box (no shadow) so the shadow stays meaningful.
