The brand's standalone device - the wordmark's full stop, used as avatar, watermark or section punctuation where the full name won't fit.

```jsx
<PunchDot size={64} />
<PunchDot size={64} variant="avatar" />
<PunchDot size={20} color="var(--paper)" />  {/* on a jacaranda ground */}
```

The SVG originals live in `assets/logo/punch-dot.svg` and `assets/logo/avatar.svg` - prefer those for favicons and social avatars. Note: it is a square, not a circle. Nothing in this system is rounded.
