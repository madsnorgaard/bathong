The share.

Most people will meet Bathong as a link in a WhatsApp group, not as a homepage. That preview card is the first thing the collective ever shows them, and right now it is the least designed surface we own. This is the system that fixes it: six card templates, the exact meta block, and the rules that keep a photograph looking like a photograph when a platform crops it.

00 / why this matters more than it looks

The card is the front door

This collective already runs on WhatsApp. So does most of South Africa, and so will most of our sharing: a member sends a photocall to a group, a curator forwards an essay, someone drops the walk link in a camera-club chat. The preview card is doing the work a homepage never gets the chance to do.

The strict one

WhatsApp decides in kilobytes

WhatsApp chooses between the big banner preview and a tiny thumbnail mostly on image size - too small, too heavy, or not landscape enough and it falls back to the thumbnail. The 600 KB cap is the single most common reason previews break.

Our rule: 1200×630 JPEG, quality 82, under 300 KB. No exceptions.

The crop

Design for the centre 80%

WhatsApp centre-crops anything that is not 1.91:1, so key visuals and text must sit inside the centre 80%. Our wordmark bar is full width but its content is inset 40px, and the title never runs to the edge.

The cache

You get one shot

WhatsApp caches previews for days or weeks and there is no official way to force a refresh. A query parameter on the URL is the workaround. Practically: get the card right before the link goes in the group, because the group will see the first version for a fortnight.

Right now

bathong.africa currently ships a description and a title but no og:image. Every link shared to date has rendered as a bare grey text row. That is the cheapest, highest-impact fix on the whole site and it can ship this week, before any of the v2 build.

01 / the rules

Five rules, then the templates

01

The photograph is the card

A share card is a frame with a bar under it. It is never a poster with a photo behind the type. Brand furniture sits below the image on its own ink plate - colour is never laid over the work, exactly as everywhere else in the system.

02

Credit travels

Every card carrying a photograph carries © Photographer. A card is the most-copied object we make, and an uncredited frame in circulation is the one failure this collective cannot have.

03

One line of display type, maximum

The card is read at 300px wide in a chat bubble. A headline of more than five words is unreadable. The essay title goes in og:title, where the platform sets it in its own type - not burned into the image twice.

04

The jacaranda rule bar

A 4px jacaranda rule between the photograph and the ink bar is the one piece of consistent brand furniture across all six cards. At thumbnail size it is the only thing that reads as us, and it costs nothing.

05

No card without a photograph, except one

The only type-only card is the site default, and it exists purely as a fallback for pages with no image yet. Every other card is led by a frame. If there is no frame, the page is not ready to be shared.

04 / the meta block

Ship this

Goes in <head> of every page. Per-page values in the marked lines; everything else is constant.

Watch

One og:image tag only

Multiple og:image tags cause unpredictable behaviour - WhatsApp typically takes the first it finds, and some readers take the last. Emit exactly one.

Watch

Absolute HTTPS URLs

The page must be HTTPS and og:image must be an absolute URL, or the preview is silently dropped. A relative path is the second most common failure after file size.

Watch

Do not ship WebP here

WebP is right for the site's own frames and wrong for og:image - support is unreliable across preview crawlers. JPEG at 80-85% keeps a 1200×630 under 200 KB and looks identical at preview size.

06 / order of work

What to do about it

This week · one hour

Ship C1

Export the site default card, put it at /share/default.jpg, add the meta block to the holding page. Every link shared from now on stops looking broken. This is worth doing before a single line of v2 is written.

With Phase 1

C4 and C5 by hand

The walk card and the photocall card are one-offs at launch. Design them as files, not as a generator. Two exports, done in an afternoon.

With Phase 2

Generate C2 and C3

Once essays and photographer pages exist there are too many to hand-make. The card becomes a rendered template: lead frame in, credit and index from the CMS, JPEG out on publish. Same six layouts, no design decisions at runtime.

Decisions I need

1. Which frame is the site default? It is the single most-seen photograph we will own and it should be a Pretoria frame, chosen by group edit, not a demo image. 2. Do we want an Instagram story template in this system now, or is the grid enough until launch?
