# BATHONG. - Full Brand & Platform Brief

**Street & documentary photography collective - Pretoria (Pitori), South Africa**
Version 1.0 - 28 July 2026 - Prepared by Mads (digital & photography) for the founding circle: Man-e, Alet, Jacques, Mads

## 1. What this is

A single reference document for the name, the brand, the website, the offer and the launch plan, so that design, registration, pricing and communication all pull in the same direction. It consolidates everything agreed in the group to date, including the 28 July meeting agenda points: business registration, tax, branding redesign, website, membership structure, next photowalk and the NPC exhibition track.

## 2. Background

The collective grows out of two established communities: Street Photography South Africa (Mads, thousands of followers) and the Pretoria street photography page founded by Man-e. The Pretoria Press Club (NPC, via Antoinette) has offered practical support: venue access including the Voortrekker Monument roof, membership card production marked "supported by NPC", and funding interest in a documentary exhibition on immigration. The group is now formalising: company registration and tax are in motion with an accountant, and the working name "Strata" was chosen and then found to be taken, which triggered the renaming below.

## 3. The name

**Bathong** (Sepedi / Setswana, from *batho* - people, with the locative *-ng*).

It carries two meanings at once, and both are the brand:

1. Literally **"among the people"** - where street and documentary photography is made.
2. Colloquially, the **exclamation of astonishment** - what you say out loud when the street shows you something unbelievable. It is the sound of the decisive moment.

It passes the street test: it is a word actually said in Pitori every day, not a word invented in a boardroom. It is local at first hearing and explainable to the world in one line.

**Punctuation system (this is a rule, not a suggestion):**

- **Bathong.** with a full stop is the wordmark. Calm, confident, gallery-grade. It goes on the site masthead, membership cards, exhibition walls and legal documents.
- **Bathong!** with the exclamation is the voice. It lives in campaign language, photocall posters, captions, the ticker, stickers and merchandise. The mark states; the street shouts.

**Status and outstanding checks:** web searches found no existing photography brand using the name. Still to do before full commitment: register the domain (bathong.co.za first choice; consider bathong.africa and bathong.com), secure the Instagram handle (working suggestion @bathong.collective), run the CIPC name search via the accountant - a compound such as "Bathong Collective NPC" will likely clear where the bare word may not, since bathong is a common word - and consider a trademark filing in class 41 (education/cultural) once budget allows.

## 4. Positioning

**One line:** Bathong is a street and documentary photography collective that starts in Pretoria and walks outward - building photographers through honest, frame-by-frame feedback, publishing stories about people as they are, and putting them on walls, from Pitori to the world.

**The model:** a collective, not a club. The reference is the great photography cooperatives - Magnum above all, and at home the lineage of Afrapix and the Market Photo Workshop: photographers owning their platform, their standards and their archive together. Membership is open to anyone; the standard of what gets published is not lowered for anyone. That tension is the engine: open door, high bar.

**Three verbs, one core.** Everything the collective does maps to three actions, and all three are primary - the website treats them as three equal doors:

- **Build** - membership, mentorship, workshops, group edits, portfolio reviews.
- **Engage** - photowalks, rooftop sessions, community events, partnerships.
- **Show** - photocalls, publication on the platform, exhibitions, the archive.

**What makes it different from every other photo club:** it publishes. Walks end in group edits; group edits feed photo essays; photo essays feed exhibitions. The feed is not the destination, the wall and the archive are.

## 5. Audiences

Primary: Pretoria-based photographers of all levels who want to grow - from phone shooters discovered through the Facebook pages to working photographers who want a collective and an exhibition track. Secondary: the existing national audience of Street Photography SA (reach, submissions, credibility). Third: partners and funders - the Press Club NPC, venues, cultural institutions, brands wanting authentic Pretoria imagery. Fourth: the global street and documentary photography community, reached through the essays and open photocalls.

## 6. Brand identity

**Wordmark.** BATHONG set in Archivo Black, uppercase, tight tracking, with the full stop in jacaranda purple. The purple punch-dot is the recurring device: it ends the wordmark, punctuates section titles, and can stand alone as an avatar or watermark at small sizes.

**Colour.** Four colours, no more:

- Ink `#141313` - near-black, the darkroom.
- Paper `#F2EEE6` - warm off-white, the print.
- Jacaranda `#7B5CD6` (deep variant `#5636B8`) - Pretoria in October. This is the identity colour; no other photography collective owns purple.
- Signal `#E8FF38` - highveld flash. Accent only: highlights, tags, the member CTA. Never a background for long text.

**Type.** Archivo Black for display (free, Google Fonts). Space Grotesk for body. Space Mono for the editorial furniture: captions, labels, metadata, dictionary entries. The mono layer is what makes it feel like contact sheets and archive cards rather than a startup landing page.

**Texture.** A fine film-grain overlay sits on everything digital. Photography-first heritage, analog soul, and it unifies mixed member imagery.

**The dictionary device.** The definition card - "ba·thong, excl./loc., 1. what you say when you can't believe what you're seeing; 2. among the people" - is a portable brand asset. It works as an Instagram slide, a wall text, a tote, the back of the membership card. It explains the name every time without a paragraph of copy.

**Photography treatment.** Member work is shown as shot - no brand filters forced onto the photographs. Brand colour lives in the frame around the work (borders, labels, tags), never on top of it. Grids keep 2px ink borders and generous spacing; captions in mono, always credited. Credit is non-negotiable everywhere the work appears.

**Don'ts.** No camera iconography (apertures, lenses, focus brackets) in the logo or graphics - the work is the imagery. No gradients on type. Never set the wordmark in another typeface. Never use the exclamation mark in the masthead or on legal/financial documents.

## 7. Voice

Confident, warm, streetwise, precise. Short sentences. Local words used naturally and unitalicised: Pitori, 012, bathong. English is the base language so the world can read it; Sepitori and Afrikaans appear the way they appear on the street - unforced. The voice teaches without lecturing: every caption is a chance to make someone a better photographer.

Examples. Event post: "Saturday, first light, three layers of the capital. Bring one lens. Bathong!" Rejection note to a submitter: "Not this time - and here is why, frame by frame. Shoot the next call." Exhibition wall text: calm, factual, in the mark's register, no exclamation.

## 8. The website

**Live v1** has been designed and built (single self-contained HTML file, mobile-ready, delivered 28 July). Architecture: Hero with the wordmark and the three equal doors (Join / Walk with us / Submit) - Manifesto with the dictionary card - Photo Stories (essay grid: Marabastad, Above the Capital rooftop series, Arrivals immigration project) - Walks & Workshops (featured: 29 Aug Layers Route) - Membership (benefits plus card visual, price marked TBC) - Exhibitions - The Collective (people and lineage) - Submit (open photocall with form).

**Content rules.** The work comes first: no page may lead with text where a photograph can lead. Every photograph credited. Photo essays are 12 to 20 frames, sequenced, with a short text - the Magnum-style story unit the whole platform is built around.

**Roadmap.** Phase 1 (now): static site live on bathong.co.za, mailto forms, Instagram and Facebook pointing at it. Phase 2 (1 to 3 months): booking and payment for walks (Quicket or Payfast), newsletter tool (Mailerlite or Substack), photocall upload flow. Phase 3 (3 to 12 months): member accounts, the searchable open archive, essay CMS so editors publish without touching code.

**Placeholders to replace before launch:** real photographs in every marked PHOTO SLOT (lead frames chosen by group edit), final membership price, confirmed email address, live social handles, walk booking link.

## 9. The offer

**Membership - anyone can join.** Benefits as agreed: member pricing and priority booking on workshops and photowalks; photocalls with publication on the platform, fully credited; the monthly newsletter (assignments, critique notes, opportunities, calendar); a real chance to exhibit; group edits, portfolio reviews and mentorship. Physical membership cards produced with "supported by NPC". Pricing is tonight's decision - market reference: commercial Joburg/Pretoria photo tours charge around R2000 per person per walk, which means even a few hundred rand annual membership plus modest per-walk fees is dramatically strong value; recommend an annual individual tier, a student/concession tier, and per-event pricing for non-members set noticeably higher than the member rate so membership always pays for itself within two or three walks.

**Programmes.** Monthly photowalks planned like assignments, each ending in a group edit (next: Saturday 29 August 2026, the Layers Route - Rooftops, Salvokop, Marabastad). Rooftop sessions with the Press Club NPC, starting with the Voortrekker Monument at sunrise or sunset. Inclusive workshops - the programme for children with disabilities co-led by Johan Meintjies, from lived experience. Exhibition track: the NPC-funded immigration project ("Arrivals - Stories of Immigration", working title) and the Press Club exhibition.

## 10. Launch sequence

Week 1: lock name checks (domain, IG handle, CIPC search), confirm membership pricing, drop real photographs into the site, go live. Weeks 3 to 5: drive everything to the 29 August walk as the first flagship event; capture it well - it is also the brand shoot. September: publish Essay 001 from the walk and the photocall, open membership formally with cards, begin the immigration exhibition workflow with the NPC.
