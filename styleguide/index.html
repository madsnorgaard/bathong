The name.

Sec 00 / Why this word

Bathong — Sepedi / Setswana, from batho, people, with the locative -ng. Literally among the people: where street and documentary photography is made. Colloquially, the exclamation you let out when the street shows you something unbelievable — the sound of the decisive moment.

It passes the street test: a word actually said in Pitori every day, not one invented in a boardroom. Local at first hearing, explainable to the world in one line. Say it in captions unitalicised, like Pitori and 012 — these are not foreign words here.

bathong! excl. / loc.
1. what you say when you can't believe what you're seeing.
2. ba·tho·ng: among the people — where this work is made.
3. a collective of photographers from Pretoria, for the world.

Sec 04 / The work comes first
  • P/01
    Shown as shotNo brand filters forced onto photographs. Grading choices belong to the photographer and the group edit.
  • P/02
    Colour lives in the frameBorders, labels, tags — never on top of the work. The 2px ink border is the brand's grip on the image.
