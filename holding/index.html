Pitori · 012 Street & documentary photography collective Est. 2026

A new collective  Pretoria, South Africa

Bathong.

The word you say out loud when the street shows you something unbelievable. We are building a collective of photographers who are there when it happens.

Street and documentary photography from the capital - built by its photographers, published as photo stories, shown on walls. From Pitori to the world.

What we are building
  • 01
    BuildMembership, group edits, mentorship - photographers making photographers.
  • 02
    EngagePhotowalks and rooftop sessions - the city, shot together.
  • 03
    ShowPhotocalls, photo essays, exhibitions - the wall is the destination, not the feed.
